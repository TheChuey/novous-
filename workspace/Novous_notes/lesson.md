Lesson Plan:

Critical Thinking Strategies for Business Decisions

Objectives:

Examine the process involved with problem formulation
Determine factors affecting decision implementation
Apply critical thinking strategies to business decisions

Materials:

Week 1 course materials
Video on critical thinking
Jungian type and Keirsey Temperament assessments
Case studies on business decisions

Procedure:

1. Read week 1 course materials and watch the video on critical thinking
2. Determine your Jungian type and Keirsey Temperament
3. Analyze case studies on business decisions and apply critical thinking strategies
4. Write a reflection paper on the importance of critical thinking in business

Assessment:

Participation in class discussions (20%)
Reflection paper (30%)
Case study analysis (30%)
Final exam (20%)

Timeline:

Week 1-2: Read course materials and watch video on critical thinking
Week 3-4: Determine Jungian type and Keirsey Temperament
Week 5-6: Analyze case studies and apply critical thinking strategies
Week 7: Write reflection paper and participate in class discussions
